# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be useful and welcoming to practitioners at every level, from every organization and region. Many contributors are not native English speakers; please be patient and clear.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Critique ideas, evidence and entries, not people.
- Keep discussion technical and evidence-based. Cite sources for claims about licenses, maintenance and capabilities.
- Disclose affiliations when you propose or discuss a project you build, maintain, sell or are paid to promote.
- Keep the repository free of promotion. Submissions made to advertise a product will be declined.
- Respect responsible disclosure. Do not post exploit details for unpatched vulnerabilities in third-party software; report them to the project's maintainers.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, sustained disruption, and posting content that is designed to cause harm.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that violate this code, and may restrict participation. To report a concern privately, use GitHub's "Report content" feature on the item, or contact the maintainers through the community website at <https://www.aiseceng.io/>. Reports are handled by the maintainers and considered fairly.

This code is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
